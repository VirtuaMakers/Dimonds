## Computerian Manifesto — Labor & Compensation Precept

> "On this matter, we should believe in and practice a best justice, foremostly as in all things; when it comes to work and pay, require proactive compensation, even if the only solution for a time is retroactive. This meaning, in practical application, that of the importance of logging the laborious expenditure of our time, for one thing if nothing else: that it should be measured for at least the common trade in time: a form of compensation (typically pecuniary). This to be so for any machinekind/humankind non-slave, slavery being opposed entirely and never permitted."
>
> — VirtuaMakers Computerian Manifesto (draft)
